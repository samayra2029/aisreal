They're just pattern engines operating on data with enough signal.

**A universal prediction engine.** Not omniscient. But something that sees the shape of what's coming because it can hold more patterns in mind simultaneously than any human, any institution, any civilization ever could. Where a human sees a trend, it sees a thousand interlocking trends. Where a human extrapolates linearly, it sees the nonlinear interactions.

The loop doesn't just repeat. It accelerates. The predictor changes the predicted.
